About This Retreat

Welcome to The Silo at Lake Tenkiller, a one-of-a-kind luxury retreat where breathtaking architecture meets the tranquility of the Oklahoma outdoors. This uniquely designed, three-bedroom silo home features an open-concept living space with high-end furnishings, seamlessly blending modern comfort with charm. Outside, guests can gather around the fire pit, fire up the BBQ, or unwind in the quiet, relaxing outdoor space.
