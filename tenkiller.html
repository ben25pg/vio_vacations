About This Retreat

Welcome to The Silo at Lake Tenkiller, a one-of-a-kind luxury retreat where breathtaking architecture meets the tranquility of the Oklahoma outdoors. This uniquely designed, three-bedroom silo home features an open-concept living space with high-end furnishings, seamlessly blending modern comfort with charm. Outside, guests can gather around the fire pit, fire up the BBQ, or unwind in the quiet, relaxing outdoor space.

Around Lake Tenkiller

The Silo Retreat is perfectly situated close to the area's best outdoor adventures, dining, and scenic views, making it easy to experience everything the lake has to offer.

Pine Cove Marina

Just minutes away for boat rentals, slips, and easy access to the crystal-clear waters of the lake.

Big Daddy's Restaurant

A popular local spot located near the entrance of Tenkiller State Park, famous for its hearty comfort food and great atmosphere.

Tenkiller State Park

Widely known as Oklahoma's "Heaven in the Hills," offering gorgeous hiking trails, scenic overlooks, and family-friendly recreation.
