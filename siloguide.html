Curated Recommendations

Area Guide

Dining Out

Where to Eat

Big Daddy’s Restaurant

Just down the road for hearty comfort food and classic lakeside burgers.

Clearwater Cafe

Conveniently located right inside Tenkiller State Park.

El Lago Mexican Grill

Located in Gore, great for family dining and Mexican cuisine.

Rustic Cantina

A local favorite situated in nearby Vian.

Gambino's & Simple Simon's

Perfect options for quick pizza and family-friendly meals in Gore and Vian.

The Kove & Stoneridge

Great local dining atmospheres at The Kove (Gore) and Stoneridge (Paradise Hill).

Adventure & Leisure

Things to Do

Tenkiller State Park

Enjoy fantastic swimming areas, hiking trails, and scenic overlooks.

Marina Boat Rentals

Rent watercraft easily with nearby access at Pine Cove Marina and Strayhorn Marina.

Tenkiller Thriller Water Park

An all-new inflatable water park located right at Burnt Cabin Marina.

Illinois River Float

Experience popular tube floating down the scenic Illinois River.

World-Class Fishing

Cast your line nearby at Lake Tenkiller, the Illinois River (trout), or the Arkansas River.

Bait & Tackle Supplies

Stock up on fishing essentials at Gore Bait and Tackle or Harps in Gore.

Local Supplies

Shopping & Groceries

Groceries & Essentials

Stock your vacation fridge easily:

  • Harps Food Stores (Gore and Vian)
  • Dollar General (HWY 100 & 82)

Local Boutiques & Shops

Explore local stores in Gore:

  • The Kove
  • Anne Paige
  • Fabric Patch
