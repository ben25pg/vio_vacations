The Ultimate Hilltop Sanctuary

About This Retreat

A luxury hilltop estate designed for groups who want it all.

Perched on Circle Rd with sweeping panoramic views of the Hill Country and Lake LBJ, this 4-bedroom sanctuary offers more than just a place to sleep—it’s essentially a private resort. Whether you are floating in the private pool watching the sunset, challenging friends in the game room, or hosting a private screening in the cinema, this home is built for entertainment. Perfect for family reunions, golf groups, and couples' retreats looking for the ultimate Horseshoe Bay experience.

The Entertainment Wing

Rain or shine, the fun never stops. Enjoy a dedicated Home Theater with cinema seating and a fully equipped Game Room.

The Private Oasis

Escape the crowds. Backyard features a sparkling private pool and ample deck space with 180-degree views that turn golden at sunset.

Luxury Living

High-end finishes, an open-concept chef’s kitchen, and spacious sleeping quarters ensure 5-star comfort for your entire group.

Inside the Home

  • Living & Dining: Step into a grand open-concept living space bathed in natural light. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame the stunning landscape, bringing the outdoors in.
  • Chef’s Kitchen: Fully stocked with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and everything you need to cook a holiday feast.
  • Gathering Spaces: Plush seating, large smart TV, and a dining area designed for long dinners and good conversation.

The "Fun" Zones (Premium Amenities)

  • Home Theater: Why go out? Sink into reclining theater seats and enjoy a movie night with incredible sound and picture quality.
  • Game Room: The ultimate hangout spot featuring air hockey, shuffleboard, indoor cornhole, and board games.
  • The Pool Deck: The crown jewel of the property. Lounge on the patio, grill up a BBQ dinner, and take a dip while overlooking the Texas Hill Country.
  • The Tree House: Escape to the backyard treehouse, a shaded and peaceful retreat perfect for reading or afternoon daydreams.

Bedrooms & Bathrooms

Sleeps up to 8 Guests comfortably.

  • Primary Suite: King Bed, en-suite spa bathroom, and direct view access.
  • Bedroom 2: Queen Bed – High-quality linens, ample storage, and lake view.
  • Bedroom 3: Queen Bed – High-quality linens. Perfect for couples.
  • Bedroom 4: Two twin beds – A haven for the kids or single travelers.

Location & Activities

Located in the heart of Horseshoe Bay, close to the best the area has to offer:

  • Golf: Minutes from Slick Rock, Apple Rock, and Ram Rock courses.
  • Lake Life: A short drive to Lake LBJ marinas for boat rentals and jet skis.
  • Wine & Dine: Easy access to Marble Falls (10 mins) and the famous 290 Wine Trail (45 mins).
