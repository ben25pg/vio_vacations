Curated Recommendations

Horseshoe Bay Guide

Dining Out

Where to Eat

Bluebonnet Cafe

A legendary Texas staple in nearby Marble Falls, famous for its all-day comfort food breakfasts and incredible daily pie happy hour.

On the Rocks

Enjoy a laid-back dining experience with great American fare, craft cocktails, and stunning panoramic views of Lake LBJ.

Bear King Brewing Co.

Located in Marble Falls, offering fantastic craft beers, great food, and an open-air patio perfect for relaxing with family and friends.

LeStourgeon Seafood Co.

A hidden gem in Cottonwood Shores serving up fantastic, fresh seafood dishes with a welcoming, unpretentious atmosphere.

Pizza Mia

A highly-rated local spot in Cottonwood Shores, perfect when you want a quick, easy, and delicious family pizza night after a long day.

Julie's Cocina

A local favorite just down the road, serving up homestyle Tex-Mex with excellent house-made margaritas and generous portions.

Adventure & Leisure

Things to Do

Public Boat Rentals

Rent a pontoon boat, wakeboard boat, or jet skis from public spots like Splash Boat Rentals to explore the constant-level waters of Lake LBJ.

Inks Lake State Park

Just a short drive away, this beautiful state park offers excellent public hiking trails, swimming holes, and family-friendly outdoor recreation.

Longhorn Cavern State Park

Take a guided underground walking tour of this unique Texas cave system, perfect for a cool, shady afternoon adventure.

Sweet Berry Farm

A fantastic seasonal attraction in Marble Falls where families can pick fresh strawberries, navigate mazes, and enjoy local farm activities.

Hidden Falls Adventure Park

Rent an ATV or bring your own off-road vehicle to explore miles of rugged, scenic Texas Hill Country trails.

Hill Country Wine Tasting

Explore the nearby Texas Wine Trail, featuring stunning public vineyards and tasting rooms just outside of town.

Local Supplies

Shopping & Groceries

Bayside Fresh Market

Located right on FM 2147, this market is completely open to the public and perfect for stocking your vacation fridge.

  • High-quality meat and seafood.
  • Artisan goods, fresh produce, and fine European cheeses.
  • A massive selection of over 300 wines.

Downtown Marble Falls

Head into town to explore the historic Main Street shopping district:

  • Charming local clothing and home decor boutiques.
  • Unique antique shops and local art galleries.
  • Specialty stores like Choccolatte's for handmade treats.
