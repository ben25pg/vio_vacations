The Luxury Hilltop Horseshoe Bay

About This Retreat

Experience Elevated Lakefront Living at Horseshoe Bay.

Perched high above the water to capture breathtaking, panoramic views, The Luxury Hilltop HB is a masterclass in coastal sophistication. Whether you are seeking a serene escape from the everyday or a spacious haven for friends and family, our retreat provides the perfect balance of refined luxury and effortless comfort. Wake up to the gentle light of the morning shimmering across the bay, then step out onto your expansive private deck to enjoy your coffee with a view. Inside, you’ll find thoughtfully curated interiors—a seamless blend of modern elegance and cozy warmth designed to make you feel right at home the moment you arrive. From quiet evenings watching the sunset from the hilltop to adventurous days on the water just minutes from your front door, this is more than just a stay—it is the destination. Experience the pinnacle of Horseshoe Bay living, where every detail is crafted to ensure your time here is nothing short of extraordinary.
